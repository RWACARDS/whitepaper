## Plan rules

- The plan is valid for 3 years and is renewed by purchasing it again at its full price for the next 3 years - the terms are on the [Limits and Fees](/en/limits-and-fees) page.
- You can upgrade to a higher plan at any time: the same card with the new plan's limits. On an upgrade to Black or Signature the physical card is added.
- Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the plan.
- The virtual card is issued right after payment and activated by the first top-up of $20 or more; the physical one is delivered to you and activated from your account on arrival.
- Verification is passed once - after that your account can hold several cards: each has its own balance and its own limits from its plan.
