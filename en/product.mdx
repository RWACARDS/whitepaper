## Where it works

**175,000,000+** merchant locations in **200+** countries - everywhere Visa is accepted. Online and offline, contactless via Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal, with <Tooltip tip="Online payment confirmation with a code: an extra layer of protection.">3DS</Tooltip> payment confirmation. The card currency is US dollars.

## Tier rules

- You can upgrade to a higher tier at any time.
- Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the tier.
- The virtual card is issued right after payment; the physical one is delivered to you and activated from your account on arrival.
- Verification is passed once - after that your account can hold several cards: each has its own balance and its own limits from its tier.

A card can also be given as a gift: a gift certificate is a paid card in the form of a code. Certificates are covered further on in this document.
