## Plan rules

- Your Plan runs for three years; after that you renew it at its then-current price and receive a new card - the terms are on the [Limits and Fees](/en/limits-and-fees) page.
- You can upgrade to a higher Plan at any time: the same card and card number with the new Plan's limits. On an upgrade to Black or Signature the physical card is produced and delivered in addition to your existing virtual card; when you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added.
- Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the Plan.
- The virtual card is issued right after payment and activated by the first top-up of $20 or more; the physical one is delivered to you and activated from your account on arrival.
- Verification is passed once - after that your account can hold several cards: each has its own balance and its own limits from its Plan. Cards are personal: they may be used only by the verified holder.
