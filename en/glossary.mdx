<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Plan and card">
    - **Plan** - a [product of the platform](/en/product) purchased for three years: Standard, Premium, Black or Signature. It includes the card, its limits and services; the Plans differ in price, card type and limits.
    - **Virtual card** - a card with no physical carrier: details live in your account, payments run online and via Apple Pay / Google Pay. It is issued right after payment and does not withdraw cash.
    - **Physical card** - plastic (Black) or metal (Signature): issued in addition to the virtual card, delivered by mail, withdraws cash at ATMs. Its PIN arrives only through a one-time link sent to your verified email.
    - **Activation** - a new card is activated by its first top-up of $20 or more; a physical card is activated from your account on arrival, with a code sent to your email.
    - **Top-up** - crediting the card with USDT or USDC: the minimum is $20, the fee is 3.5% on Standard and 2% on the other Plans.
    - **Freeze** - a card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security; it is unfrozen via support.
    - **Upgrade** - moving to a higher Plan; available at any time. The same card and card number with the new Plan's limits; the price is the fixed upgrade price from the table on the [Limits and Fees](/en/limits-and-fees) page.
    - **Reissue** - a replacement card issued at a fixed price: \$30 for a virtual card, \$150 for plastic, \$300 for metal.
    - **Renewal** - the purchase of the next three-year Plan term at its then-current price; a new card is issued. Without renewal the card is deactivated.
    - **Gift certificate** - a [paid Plan in the form of a code](/en/gift-certificates): the recipient activates it, passes verification and gets the card. Valid for 12 months from purchase.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Verification and security">
    - **KYC** - verification: an identity check by document and selfie, a mandatory requirement of the banking system for issuing a card. It is passed once, from your account, with no video and no proof of address.
    - **3-D Secure (3DS)** - online payment confirmation with a code: an extra layer of protection. The code arrives by email and is also visible in your account.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Cryptocurrency">
    - **BEP-20** - the BNB Chain network: accepted for USDT and USDC top-ups.
    - **TRC-20** - the TRON network: accepted for USDT top-ups only.
    - **USDT** - a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar: top-up on the BEP-20 and TRC-20 networks.
    - **USDC** - a stablecoin pegged to the US dollar: top-up on the BEP-20 network only. A transfer on any other network will not arrive - choose the network carefully.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
