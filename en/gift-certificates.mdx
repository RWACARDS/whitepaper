You buy a certificate of the chosen tier and hand over the code - the recipient activates it on the site, passes verification and gets the card.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Buy a certificate of the tier you want - with **cryptocurrency** or from your **[Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program) rewards balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over">
    Get the code and pass it on - in person or in a message, whichever works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    The recipient activates the code on **rwanftcards.com**, passes the [standard verification](/en/how-to-get-your-card) - and the card is theirs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Verification is mandatory for the recipient, just like with a regular purchase, and the one-active-card-per-verified-identity rule applies here too.

## Who it is for

### A gift that works for you

If the recipient has not been part of the ecosystem yet - on activation they join your structure as invited by you. If they already have a sponsor or a card - the certificate stays simply a gift.

### A partner's tool

A certificate is a way to hand a client a card that is already paid for: you buy the certificate and pass on the code, and all the client has to do is activate it. The sale counts as yours at the moment the recipient activates the certificate - structure volume and Royalty Program rewards trigger right then.

> **Guard the code.** A certificate is a code: treat it like money and pass it only to the person it is meant for.
