You buy a certificate of the chosen Plan and hand over the code - the recipient activates it on the site, passes verification and gets the card. A gift certificate is valid for 12 months from purchase.

## How it works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Buy a certificate of the Plan you want - with **cryptocurrency** or, if you hold a card of your own, from your **[Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program) rewards balance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Hand over">
    Get the code and pass it on - in person or in a message, whichever works.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate">
    The recipient activates the code on **rwanftcards.com**, passes the [standard verification](/en/how-to-get-your-card) - and the card is theirs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Verification is mandatory for the recipient, just like with a regular purchase.

## Who it is for

### A gift that works for you

Buying a certificate is your personal sale - your rank percentage is credited when the recipient activates it. This works only if the recipient is new and joins your structure. If the recipient already has a sponsor, they stay with that sponsor and no one earns on this certificate. Activating a certificate on yourself is a purchase, not a sale - the reward goes up the line to your sponsor.

### A partner's tool

A certificate is a way to hand a client a Plan that is already paid for: you buy the certificate and pass on the code, and all the client has to do is activate it. The sale counts as yours at the moment the recipient activates the certificate - structure volume and Royalty Program rewards trigger right then.

> **Guard the code.** A certificate is a code: treat it like money and pass it only to the person it is meant for.
