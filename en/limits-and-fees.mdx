Plan term: 3 years, maintenance: \$0 per month and \$0 per year - the same on every Plan.

When you hold several cards, limits are counted per card - by that card's Plan. Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the Plan. Cash withdrawals at ATMs are available only with the physical Black and Signature cards; virtual cards do not withdraw cash. Limits are tied to the Plan - there is no individual raising: higher Plan, higher limits. You can [upgrade](/en/product) at any time.

## Upgrades

An upgrade keeps your card and your card number: the same card with the new Plan's limits. On an upgrade to Black or Signature the physical card - plastic or metal - is produced and delivered in addition to your existing virtual card. When you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added.

## Payments and fees

- **Maintenance** - \$0 per month and \$0 per year for the entire Plan term. No subscription.
- **[Top-up](/en/top-up-and-everyday-use)** - 3.5% on Standard, 2% on the other Plans. The minimum top-up is \$20; smaller amounts are not lost - they accumulate until the total reaches \$20.
- **Card transactions** - a fixed $0.30 fee on every card transaction. A cross-border fee of about 0.5% applies when the merchant is outside the card's issuing jurisdiction or the payment is not in USD.
- **Inactivity** - a card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security and unfrozen via support. After 12 months of inactivity a fee of up to $5 per month may apply.
- **Activation deposit** - a new card is activated by its first top-up of at least $20.
- **Physical card delivery** - included in the Plan price.
- **Balance and settlement** - in US dollars. Payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion.

## Free of charge

- **Registration and verification** - cost nothing. If anyone asks you for money to register or to pass verification, they are scammers.
- **Card issuance** - the cards included in your Plan are issued with no separate fee: issuance is already included in the Plan price. Only a replacement card is charged, at the reissue price above.

## Plan term and renewal

Your Plan runs for three years. The card itself is issued for five - the Plan term is set by us and does not depend on the date printed on the card. After three years you renew the Plan at its then-current price and receive a new card; the old one is frozen. We remind you a month before. If the Plan is not renewed, the card is deactivated.
