Plan term: 3 years, maintenance: $0 - the same on every plan. The card itself is issued for 5 years: the plan term does not depend on the expiry date printed on the card.

When you hold several cards, limits are counted per card - by that card's plan. Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the plan. Limits are tied to the plan - there is no individual raising: higher plan, higher limits. You can [upgrade](/en/product) at any time.

## Upgrades

An upgrade keeps your card: the same card with the new plan's limits. On an upgrade to Black or Signature the physical card - plastic or metal - is issued and delivered to you.

## Payments and fees

- **Maintenance** - $0 for the entire plan term. No subscription, no tokens to hold.
- **[Top-up](/en/top-up-and-everyday-use)** - the only recurring fee: 3.5% on Standard, 2% on the other plans. The minimum top-up is $20; send less and the transfer is not lost - it is credited together with your next top-up.
- **Minimum activation deposit** - \$20: after purchase the card is activated by the first top-up of \$20 or more.
- **Physical card delivery** - included in the plan price.
- **Balance and settlement** - in US dollars. Payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion.
- **[Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program) withdrawals** - weekly, from $5, in USDT to your wallet on the BEP-20 network - only while you hold an active card of your own. Topping up your own card from the rewards balance carries the standard top-up fee; the network fee is paid by the platform.

## No extra charges

- **The application and your waitlist spot** - applying and holding your place in the queue cost nothing. If anyone asks money for a place in the queue, they are scammers.
- **Joining the Royalty Program** - no entry fees, and ranks are not for sale.
- **Card issuance** - there is no separate issuance fee: it is already included in the plan price.

## Plan term and renewal

The plan is valid for 3 years. Renewal is the purchase of the plan at its full price for the next 3 years, and the card is reissued with it. The platform reminds you a month before the term ends. Without renewal the card is disabled.
