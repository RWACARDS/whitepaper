Card term: 3 years, maintenance: $0 - the same on every tier.

Limits apply equally to the virtual and physical card of the tier. Limits are tied to the tier - there is no individual raising: higher tier, higher limits. You can [upgrade](/en/product) at any time.

## Payments and fees

- **Maintenance** - $0 for the entire card term. No subscription, no tokens to hold.
- **[Top-up](/en/top-up-and-everyday-use)** - the only recurring fee: 2.8% on Standard, 2% on the other tiers. The minimum top-up is $20; send less and the transfer is not lost - it is credited together with your next top-up.
- **Physical card delivery** - included in the tier price.
- **Balance and settlement** - in US dollars. Payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion.
- **[Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program) withdrawals** - weekly, from $5, in USDT to your wallet on the BEP-20 network.

## No extra charges

- **The application and your waitlist spot** - applying and holding your place in the queue cost nothing. If anyone asks money for a place in the queue, they are scammers.
- **Joining the Royalty Program** - no entry fees, and ranks are not for sale.
- **Card issuance** - there is no separate issuance fee: it is already included in the tier price.

Card renewal terms are published in advance, before the card term ends.
