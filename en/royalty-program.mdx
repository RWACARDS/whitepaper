As your team grows, so does your share: **up to 40%** on every sale.

## Ranks and percentages

| Rank | Personal sale | Team volume |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Starter | **12%** | no requirement |
| 2. Agent | **17%** | $2,500 |
| 3. Manager | **21%** | $7,500 |
| 4. Pro Manager | **24%** | $25,000 |
| 5. Director | **26%** | $125,000 |
| 6. Pro Director | **29%** | $250,000 |
| 7. Chief Director | **32%** | $500,000 |
| 8. Vice President | **34%** | $1,000,000 |
| 9. President | **36%** | $2,000,000 |
| 10. Founder | **40%** | $5,000,000 |

A rank is granted when both conditions are met - team volume and team:

- **Manager** - 2 Starter or 1 Agent
- **Pro Manager** - 2 Agent or 1 Manager
- **Director** - 3 Manager or 1 Pro Manager
- **Pro Director** - 3 Pro Manager or 1 Director
- **Chief Director** - 3 Director or 1 Pro Director
- **Vice President** - 3 Pro Director or 1 Chief Director
- **President** - 2 Chief Director or 1 Vice President
- **Founder** - 3 Vice President or 2 President

The matching bonus unlocks at Pro Manager - details below.

## You earn the difference

Every rank has its own personal sale percentage. Your share of a sale deep in the team is the difference between your percentage and what has already been paid out below. It works at any depth: the system distributes percentages up the chain - up to the full 40%. The bigger your lead over your team, the bigger your share of every sale it makes.

## Three situations with real numbers

### Personal sale

You are a Chief Director (32%), and your client bought a Black card for \$499 - you earned about **\$160** the moment they paid.

### A sale deep in the team

A Starter-rank partner sold a Black card for $499:

| Who is in the chain | Percentage | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Starter - the seller | 12% - their personal rate | **~$60** |
| The Manager above | difference 21% - 12% = 9% | **~$45** |
| You, a Founder | difference 40% - 21% = 19% | **~$95** |
| **Total** | **40%** | **~$200** |

One sale - three people earned, and the full 40% went out along the chain.

### Growth decides

The same card was sold by a Manager, and above them sits a partner of a lower rank:

| Who is in the chain | Percentage | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| Manager - the seller | 21% | their own rate |
| The Agent above | 17% | nothing: their percentage is not higher than what is already paid out |
| Director | 26% | the 5% difference |
| Founder | 40% | the 19% difference |

The system always pays those who are ahead in rank: your team is catching up with you - **grow faster than your team**.

## Matching bonus - income from your team's income

You earn a percentage of the rewards your line partners earn - regardless of their rank, even if they have outranked you.

| Line | Your percentage | Unlocks at |
|---|---|---|
| 1st | **5%** | Pro Manager |
| 2nd | **4%** | Director |
| 3rd | **3%** | Pro Director |

A first-line partner earned \$1,000 - you automatically get \$50. At team scale, with 10 partners in each line and each of them earning $1,000:

- 1st line - **+$500**
- 2nd line - **+$400**
- 3rd line - **+$300**

> A total of **+$1,200** from your team's earnings.

## Rewards

Credited instantly, in USDT, with every sale - and the rank recalculates right away. From there, three paths:

- **Withdraw** - weekly, from $5, BEP-20 network.
- **Top up your own card.**
- **Spend inside the platform** - on a tier upgrade or a [gift certificate](/en/gift-certificates).

## Rules worth knowing

- Team volume accumulates for life and never resets - your career rank never expires.
- Team requirements count personally invited partners with an active card.
- An active card means an active referral link. What you have earned is yours forever.
- There is no cap: neither on team depth nor on total rewards.
