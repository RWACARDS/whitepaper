The cards platform is a project of the RWA NFTFI ecosystem. Here is how sponsors and referral codes work on it - from the waitlist to the launch of the [Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program).

## Why register early

New people come only through your referral link - and it appears after you [register](/en/how-to-get-your-card). Plus your invite counter and queue position start working for you from day one.

## New to the ecosystem?

You are free: enter an inviter's code or continue without one on [the waitlist](/en/how-to-get-your-card). At the sales launch a sponsor is needed to purchase - assigned automatically or by code.

## Sponsors and codes: nothing to worry about

The Royalty Program starts from zero on September 29, 2026 - together with sales and its own marketing. The waitlist does not decide your place in it: submitting an application does not put you into the partner structure, which forms the moment the program opens.

Apply either way:

- no sponsor - apply;
- a link was sent to you and you never opened it - apply;
- your sponsor has not confirmed their email yet - apply.

None of this blocks your application, and none of it costs anything. A sponsor code can be added at any point - from today until the launch, and on the launch day itself. For the launch we are preparing a separate mechanism so that every referral link is recorded correctly and every connection is fixed fairly; we will explain how it works closer to the date.

**Every confirmed application immediately gets a personal referral code and link.** That code becomes your working code at launch: the links you send today do not expire.
