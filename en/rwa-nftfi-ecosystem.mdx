The cards platform is a project of the RWA NFTFI ecosystem. Here is how sponsors and referral codes work on it - from the waitlist to the launch of the [Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program).

## Why register early

New people come only through your referral link - and it appears after you [register](/en/how-to-get-your-card). Your sponsor is fixed the moment you register with their link or code - or, if you joined the waitlist without a code, the moment you enter it in your profile. The team you build before launch is your team on day one. Plus your invite counter and queue position start working for you from the first day.

## New to the ecosystem?

You are free to apply with an inviter's code or without one - [the waitlist](/en/how-to-get-your-card) does not require it. The purchase does: before you buy, a sponsor must be linked to your account. Joined the waitlist without a code? Enter your inviter's code in your profile now - at any time before purchase. People who join after the launch also need an invitation code from a cardholder during the first months.

## Sponsors and codes: nothing to worry about

The Royalty Program starts from zero on September 29, 2026 - together with sales and its own marketing. Your place in it is fixed from the waitlist: the sponsor-referral connection is recorded at registration or when the code is entered in the profile, and the structure built on the waitlist becomes the Royalty Program structure the moment it opens.

Apply either way:

- no sponsor - apply;
- a link was sent to you and you never opened it - apply;
- your sponsor has not confirmed their email yet - apply.

None of this blocks your application, and none of it costs anything. What it does block is the purchase: a Plan is sold only from an account with a sponsor linked. Add your sponsor's code in your profile at any point before you buy - today, or on the day of purchase.

**The bridge from RWA NFTFI.** If you joined RWA NFTFI through someone's invitation, that person automatically becomes your sponsor on RWA NFT Cards - matched by your confirmed email. Only the connection carries over: turnover, ranks and structures start from zero.

**Every confirmed application immediately gets a personal referral code and link.** That code becomes your working code at launch: the links you send today do not expire.
