## The essence in plain words

Whenever anything differs, the full text of the documents prevails.

- The card is available from age 18, after identity verification. Verification is passed once: your account can then hold several cards, all issued to the verified holder. Cards are personal: only that holder may use them, and passing a card to anyone else, relatives included, is prohibited.
- Cards are issued by a licensed banking partner under a Visa license.
- Plans are developed and priced by RWA NFTFI. Their rules, term and renewal conditions are set by RWA NFTFI and do not depend on the expiry date printed on the card. By purchasing a Plan you accept its rules.
- RWA NFTFI may amend Plan conditions in response to market changes or requirements of the banking partner, with prior notice.
- The platform does not provide legal, tax or financial advice. Taxation of transactions is determined by the laws of your country.
- Personal data is processed under the Privacy Policy. User data is not shared with third parties outside an official legal procedure.
- The official website is **rwanftcards.com**; the official channels are the on-site [support chat](/en/support), support@rwanftcards.com and the accounts listed on the [Community](/en/community) page. The platform never asks for passwords, codes or documents outside the site.
