## Data

The card has no IBAN - which means it stays outside the automatic exchange of banking data between countries that regular accounts fall into. There is no name printed on the card. Verification is a light form: no video, no proof of address.

> The less data we ask for at the start, the less personal information exists to worry about at all.

Photo quality for verification is checked right on your device, at the moment of capture - before the shot goes anywhere. Your information stays confidential.

## Every day

Account access is protected in layers:

1. password plus 2FA or passkeys
2. a separate financial password for transactions
3. 3DS confirmation on online payments

Card details in your account are hidden and shown only to you, only on request - the platform does not store them in the open.

> If you notice a suspicious transaction - [contact support](/en/support) right away. And remember: the only official website is **rwanftcards.com**, and the list of things the platform will never ask for lives on the [Support page](/en/support).
