The card has no IBAN - which means it stays outside the automatic exchange of banking data between countries that regular accounts fall into. There is no name printed on the card. Verification is a light form: no video, no proof of address.

<div style={{ margin: '1.75rem 0', paddingLeft: '1.25rem', borderLeft: '2px solid #FFFFFF', fontSize: '1.05rem', color: 'rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)' }}>
  The less data we ask for at the start, the less personal information exists to worry about at all.
</div>

Photo quality for verification is checked right on your device, at the moment of capture - before the shot goes anywhere. Your information stays confidential.
