<Steps>
  <Step title="Apply">
    Go to **rwanftcards.com**, enter your email and your name in Latin letters exactly as in your passport, and confirm your email with a code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify">
    A light form: no video and no proof of address.

    - Personal details
    - A document
    - Three photos - the document from both sides and a selfie holding it

    Examples of correct photos are right in the form, so you pass on the first try. The most common mistakes with photos are covered in the [announcement](https://news.rwanftfi.com/the-kyc-form-now-checks-your-photos-in-real-time-600-applications-already-in) about the KYC form.

    > Approval first - payment after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get your card">
    Choose a tier and pay with <Tooltip tip="A cryptocurrency pegged to the US dollar. The card supports USDT and USDC.">stablecoins</Tooltip> - or activate a [gift certificate](/en/gift-certificates). The virtual card is issued within **5 minutes**. On the Signature and Black tiers the physical card - plastic or metal - is delivered to you and activated from your account on arrival.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From there - you simply use it: top-up and everyday payments are covered on the [Top-Up and Everyday Use](/en/top-up-and-everyday-use) page.
