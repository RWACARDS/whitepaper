title: "How to Get Your Card"
description: "Four steps from registration to an active card"
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register">
    Go to **rwanftcards.com**, enter your email and your name in Latin letters exactly as in your passport, and confirm your email with a code.

    > **Check your email provider.** Emails and codes from the banking partner do not reach addresses on mail.ru, yandex.ru, ukr.net, meta.ua, list.ru, online.ua and rambler.ru - register with an email from a different provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your identity">
    Start verification from your account. You do not choose a Plan at this step - that happens at purchase. A light form: no video and no proof of address.

    - Personal details
    - A document
    - Three photos - the document from both sides and a selfie holding it

    Examples of correct photos are right in the form, so you pass on the first try. The most common mistakes with photos are covered in the articles on [news.rwanftfi.com](https://news.rwanftfi.com/en).

    > Approval first - payment after.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy a Plan">
    Choose a Plan and pay with <Tooltip tip="A cryptocurrency pegged to the US dollar. The card supports USDT and USDC.">stablecoins</Tooltip> - or activate a [gift certificate](/en/gift-certificates). The virtual card is issued within **5 minutes**. On the Black and Signature Plans the physical card - plastic or metal - comes in addition: it is delivered to you and activated from your account on arrival.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Top up from $20">
    Your first top-up of $20 or more activates the card - from that moment it is ready to pay.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From there - you simply use it: top-up and everyday payments are covered on the [Top-Up and Everyday Use](/en/top-up-and-everyday-use) page.
