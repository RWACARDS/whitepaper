## What we help with

Your application and its statuses, verification questions, account and sign-in, top-up and payments, the Royalty Program and team bindings. Sponsor disputes are resolved using the system's records - every binding carries its exact time and source.

## How to get an answer faster

- Write from the email your application is on, or mention it in the message.
- Describe the situation in one or two sentences.
- Attach a screenshot if you see an error.

## What support will never ask for

This is your shield against scammers - remember four things:

- **Passwords, codes from emails and seed phrases** - never, in any form.
- **Documents in the chat** - never: documents are uploaded only through the form on the site.
- **[Card details](/en/trust-and-security)** - never.
- **A payment or a transfer** - never: support does not take payments.

> If someone claims to be support and asks for anything on this list - they are scammers, no matter how convincing the profile or the site looks. The only official website is **rwanftcards.com**, and similar domains are not ours. Send the link or a description to the real chat - we will take it from there.

## Privacy of requests

Information about an application goes only to its owner. Support discloses no user's data to anyone else.
