## Card and Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this a credit card?">
    No, a debit card: you spend only what you have topped up. There is no credit limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there hidden charges?">
    Maintenance is **\$0** for all three years of the Plan, and there is no subscription. The [top-up fee](/en/limits-and-fees) is **3.5%** on Standard and **2%** on the other Plans. Every card transaction carries a fixed \$0.30 fee. A cross-border fee of about 0.5% applies when the merchant is outside the card's issuing jurisdiction or the payment is not in USD. A card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security and unfrozen via support; after 12 months of inactivity a fee of up to \$5 per month may apply. A replacement card is reissued at a fixed price: \$30 for a virtual card, \$150 for plastic, \$300 for metal.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there cashback or interest on the balance?">
    No: the card has no cashback and no interest on the balance.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I activate my card?">
    Make your first top-up of **$20** or more: the virtual card is issued within 5 minutes of payment and becomes active as soon as that top-up lands.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after 3 years?">
    Your Plan is renewed by purchasing it again at its then-current price for the next three years. A new card is issued and the old one is frozen. Without renewal the card is deactivated. We remind you a month before the term ends.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have several cards?">
    Yes. Verification is passed once, and after it your account can hold several cards: each has its own balance and its own limits from its Plan. That makes it easy to separate budgets - everyday spending, subscriptions, travel. Cards are personal: only the verified holder may use them, and passing a card to anyone else, relatives included, is prohibited.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I raise the limits separately?">
    Limits are tied to the Plan: higher Plan, higher limits. You can [upgrade](/en/product) at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does an upgrade cost?">
    An upgrade is the same card with the new Plan's limits - you keep your card number. On Black and Signature the physical card is added; when you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added. The prices:

    - Standard to Premium - $150
    - Standard to Black - $350
    - Standard to Signature - $499
    - Premium to Black - $200
    - Premium to Signature - $499
    - Black to Signature - $400
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my name printed on the card?">
    No.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why does my card say Visa Business?">
    It names the card product type in the Visa payment network. You do not need a company or any other legal entity to hold the card.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Physical cards

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My physical card has arrived - what do I do?">
    Activate it from your account: press "I have the card" and enter the code sent to your email. After that, your PIN arrives through a one-time link sent to your verified email.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I get my PIN?">
    Only through a one-time link sent to your verified email. The PIN is never printed and never included in the envelope.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change the delivery address?">
    Yes, in your account, while its status is **Coming soon** - that is, until the card ships.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Top-up and payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I top up by bank card or bank transfer?">
    No, top-up is crypto only: USDT (BEP-20, TRC-20) and USDC (BEP-20 only).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fast does a top-up arrive?">
    Usually within minutes after network confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My crypto payment was rejected - where does the refund go?">
    To the address you specify, on the same network the payment was sent on.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay in euros and other currencies?">
    Yes. The balance is in dollars; payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion, and a cross-border fee of about 0.5% applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the card have an IBAN?">
    No, and that is part of the privacy: the card stays outside the automatic exchange of banking data. Incoming bank transfers are not accepted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw cash?">
    Yes, with the physical Black and Signature cards - up to **$3,000** per day. Virtual cards do not withdraw cash.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Account and verification

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which email can I register with?">
    With any provider except mail.ru, yandex.ru, ukr.net, meta.ua, list.ru, online.ua and rambler.ru: emails and codes from the banking partner do not reach these addresses. Register with an email from a different provider.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Which documents work?">
    An international passport - for everyone; for most nationalities a driver's license or an ID card also works. The document must be valid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a video or proof of address?">
    No. Only the form, a document and three photos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I apply in my country?">
    The list of available countries is shown in the application form; it is set by the banking partner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="From what age?">
    18 and older.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does verification from another platform or exchange count?">
    No, verification for the card is passed separately - a requirement of the banking partner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account or change my email?">
    Through [support](/en/support): write to the chat on **rwanftcards.com** or to support@rwanftcards.com.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my money if the bank has problems?">
    Funds sit in a segregated account, apart from the bank's capital.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I recognize official channels?">
    The only official website is **rwanftcards.com**, and the official accounts are listed on the [Community](/en/community) page - anything else with a similar name is not ours. The platform never asks for passwords, codes or documents outside the site.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
