## Card and Plans

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is this a credit card?">
    No, a debit card: you spend only what you have topped up. There is no credit limit.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Are there hidden charges?">
    Maintenance is **\$0** for all three years of the Plan, and there is no subscription. The [top-up fee](/en/limits-and-fees) is **3.5%** on Standard and **2%** on the other Plans. Every card transaction carries a fixed \$0.30 fee. A cross-border fee of about 0.5% applies when the merchant is outside the card's issuing jurisdiction or the payment is not in USD. A card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security and unfrozen via support; after 12 months of inactivity a fee of up to \$5 per month may apply.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is there cashback or interest on the balance?">
    No. Instead there is the [Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program): rewards for recommending the cards.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I activate my card?">
    Make your first top-up of **$20** or more: the virtual card is issued within 5 minutes of payment and becomes active as soon as that top-up lands.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens after 3 years?">
    Your Plan is renewed by purchasing it again at the current price. A new card is issued, the old one is frozen. Without renewal the card stops working. You get a reminder a month before.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I have several cards?">
    Yes. Verification is passed once, and after it your account can hold several cards: each has its own balance and its own limits from its Plan. That makes it easy to separate budgets - everyday spending, subscriptions, travel. Cards are personal: they may be used only by the verified holder - not by family, friends or anyone else.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I raise the limits separately?">
    Limits are tied to the Plan: higher Plan, higher limits. You can [upgrade](/en/product) at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How much does an upgrade cost?">
    An upgrade is the same card with the new Plan's limits - you keep your card number. On Black and Signature the physical card is added; when you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added. The prices:

    - Standard to Premium - $150
    - Standard to Black - $350
    - Standard to Signature - $499
    - Premium to Black - $200
    - Premium to Signature - $499
    - Black to Signature - $400
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is my name printed on the card?">
    No.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Top-up and payments

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can I top up by bank card or bank transfer?">
    No, top-up is crypto only: USDT (BEP-20, TRC-20) and USDC (BEP-20).
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How fast does a top-up arrive?">
    Usually within minutes after network confirmation.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I pay in euros and other currencies?">
    Yes. The balance is in dollars; payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion, and a cross-border fee of about 0.5% applies.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does the card have an IBAN?">
    No, and that is part of the privacy: the card stays outside the automatic exchange of banking data. Incoming bank transfers are not accepted.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw cash?">
    Yes, with the physical Black and Signature cards - up to **$3,000** per day.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Verification and geography

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Which documents work?">
    An international passport - for everyone; for most nationalities a driver's license or an ID card also works. The document must be valid.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Do I need a video or proof of address?">
    No. Only the form, a document and three photos.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I apply in my country?">
    The list of available countries is shown in the application form; it is set by the banking partner.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="From what age?">
    18 and older.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does verification from another platform or exchange count?">
    No, verification for the card is passed separately - a requirement of the banking partner.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Royalty Program

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I pay to join or to get a rank?">
    No. There are no fees, and ranks are not for sale - only team volume and team requirements. Rewards are paid exclusively from Plan sales.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does a rank expire?">
    No. Volume accumulates for life, and a career rank is never downgraded.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I change my sponsor?">
    The sponsor is fixed once and forever. Disputes are resolved by [support](/en/support) using the system's records.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I withdraw rewards without a card?">
    No. Rewards accrue, but you need an active card to withdraw or use them. Without a card you have 30 days from the first accrual to get one - rewards not unlocked in that time expire.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My referral bought before me - what happens to my reward?">
    It accrues and waits for your card for 30 days.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What about taxes on rewards?">
    That depends on the laws of your country - consult a tax advisor.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Security

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="What happens to my money if the bank has problems?">
    Funds sit in a segregated account, apart from the bank's capital.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I recognize official channels?">
    The only official website is **rwanftcards.com**. The platform never asks for passwords, codes or documents outside the site.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Waitlist

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Do I pay anything on the waitlist?">
    No, the application is free. If anyone asks money for a place in the queue - they are scammers.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What does the waitlist give me?">
    Verification in advance and an instant purchase on launch day, a personal referral link that already works, and a queue with early access building up.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is a referral code required?">
    Not for the waitlist application. The purchase does require a sponsor linked to your account: if you joined the waitlist without a code, enter your inviter's code in your profile now - at any time before you buy. People who join after the launch also need an invitation code from a cardholder during the first months.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How many cards are in the first wave?">
    **5,000**, and the counter of remaining spots is on the site. Your queue position gives early access to purchase.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
