## Top-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the coin and the network">
    In your account, choose the coin and the network - USDT (<Tooltip tip="Blockchain networks - BNB Chain and TRON - accepted for top-up.">BEP-20 or TRC-20</Tooltip>) or USDC (BEP-20) - and get your top-up address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the transfer">
    Send the transfer, and once it is confirmed on the network the funds land on your card - usually within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credited to your card">
    The balance is kept in US dollars.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rules are simple:

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Minimum top-up">
    **\$20**. Smaller amounts are not lost - they accumulate until the total reaches \$20.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Top-up fee">
    **3.5%** on Standard, **2%** on the other Plans.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Activation">
    Your first top-up of **$20** or more activates the card.
  </Card>
  <Card title="From rewards">
    Send your [Royalty Program](/en/royalty-program) rewards straight to your card from your account - the standard top-up fee of your Plan applies, the network fee is on us.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One coin, one network">
    The address is tied to both the coin and the network.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Crypto only">
    Bank cards and bank transfers are not available.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Soon - top-up with any popular cryptocurrency with auto-conversion to dollars: the timing is on the [Roadmap](/en/roadmap) page.

## Payments

Pay like with any Visa card - online and offline, **175,000,000+** merchant locations worldwide.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Apple Pay" icon="apple" />
  <Card title="Google Pay" icon="google" />
</Columns>

Add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay and pay with your phone. Online payments are protected with <Tooltip tip="Online payment confirmation with a code: an extra layer of protection.">3DS</Tooltip>: the confirmation code arrives by email, and the same code is visible in your account - just open the transaction. Every card transaction carries a fixed $0.30 fee. The balance is in dollars; payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion, with a cross-border fee of about 0.5%.

## Control

Every transaction is in your account history.

> If a payment is declined - you see the reason, not a guess.

An everyday tip: if subscriptions and auto-charges are tied to the card, keep a buffer on the balance to cover them.

A card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security and unfrozen via support.
