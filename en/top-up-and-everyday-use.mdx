## Top-up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the coin and the network">
    In your account, choose the coin and the network - USDT (BEP-20 or TRC-20) or USDC (BEP-20) - and get your top-up address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the transfer">
    Send the transfer, and once it is confirmed on the network the funds land on your card - usually within minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Credited to your card">
    The balance is kept in US dollars.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The rules are simple:

- The minimum top-up is **$20**. Sent less - the amount is never lost: it accumulates and is credited together with your next top-up.
- The top-up fee depends on the tier: **2.8%** on Standard, **2%** on the others.
- Send only the chosen coin and only on the chosen network - the address is tied to both.
- Top-up by bank card or bank transfer is not available - crypto only.

## Payments

Pay like with any Visa card - online and offline, **175,000,000+** merchant locations worldwide.

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Apple Pay" icon="apple" />
  <Card title="Google Pay" icon="google" />
  <Card title="PayPal" icon="paypal" />
</Columns>

Add the card to Apple Pay or Google Pay and pay with your phone; PayPal is supported too. Online payments are protected with 3DS: the confirmation code arrives by email, and the same code is visible in your account - just open the transaction. The balance is in dollars; payments in other currencies go through the payment network's conversion.

## Control

Every transaction is in your account history.

> If a payment is declined - you see the reason, not a guess.

An everyday tip: if subscriptions and auto-charges are tied to the card, keep a buffer on the balance to cover them.
