Both are contactless, and both work everywhere Visa is accepted. The virtual card is issued right after payment; the physical card always comes in addition to it - with a new Plan and on an upgrade to Black or Signature alike. When you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added. The upgrade prices are on the [Limits and Fees](/en/limits-and-fees) page.

## No name on the card

There is no cardholder name - only the brand. It is part of the [privacy approach](/en/trust-and-security): the card tells nothing about its holder.

## Visa Business on the card

The card carries the Visa Business mark: it names the card product type in the Visa payment network. You do not need a company or any other legal entity to hold the card.

## Delivery

The card arrives by mail, and the tracking number is visible in your account. Delivery is already included in the Plan price. Until the card ships, its status in your account is **Coming soon**, and you can still change the delivery address there. There is no need to wait for the package to start using the Plan: your virtual card is already issued.

## Activation

Card in hand - activate it from your account:

<Steps>
  <Step title='Press "I have the card"' />
  <Step title="Enter the code sent to your email" />
  <Step title="Get your PIN through the one-time link in the email" />
</Steps>

Until activation the card is inactive: it cannot pay, and only the verified holder can activate it. Your card PIN is delivered only through a one-time link sent to your verified email - it is never printed and never included in the envelope. Cards are personal: only the verified holder may use them, and passing a card to anyone else, relatives included, is prohibited.

## Cash

Black and Signature cards withdraw **up to $3,000 per day** at ATMs; virtual cards do not withdraw cash. [Transaction limits](/en/limits-and-fees) are the same as the Plan's virtual card: the physical card does not split them - it mirrors them.
