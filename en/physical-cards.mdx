Both are contactless, and both work everywhere Visa is accepted. When you upgrade to Black or Signature, the physical card is produced and delivered in addition to your existing virtual card; when you move from Black to Signature, your plastic card stays with you and a metal card is added. The upgrade prices are on the [Limits and Fees](/en/limits-and-fees) page.

## No name on the card

There is no cardholder name - only the brand. It is part of the [privacy approach](/en/trust-and-security): the card tells nothing about its holder.

## Delivery

The card arrives by mail, and the tracking number is visible in your account. Delivery is already included in the Plan price. Timing depends on the country and is shown when you order. There is no need to wait for the package to start using the Plan: the virtual card works from the first minute.

## Activation

Card in hand - activate it from your account:

  <Step title="Done" />

Until activation the card is inactive: it cannot pay, and only the account owner can activate it. Cards are personal: they may be used only by the verified holder - not by family, friends or anyone else. Your card PIN is delivered only through a one-time link sent to your verified email - it is never printed and never included in the envelope.

## Cash

Black and Signature withdraw **up to $3,000 per day** at ATMs. [Transaction limits](/en/limits-and-fees) are the same as the Plan's virtual card: the physical card does not split them - it mirrors them.
