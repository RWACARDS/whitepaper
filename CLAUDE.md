Product whitepaper for RWA NFT Cards - an international dollar Visa debit card funded with stablecoins. This is a product document for clients, NOT a crypto-tokenomics paper: no tokens, no tokenomics structures, ever.

Renewal (audit of 2026-09-22) = buying the Plan again at its THEN-CURRENT price for the next 3 years (never "full price"); a new card is issued and the old one is frozen; we remind a month before; without renewal the card is deactivated. The old "renewal terms are published in advance" line is retired - the terms are known and published (Product, Limits and Fees, FAQ, Glossary, Legal).
- Premium Plan: $199 (not $149).
- Plan prices: $49 / $199 / $299 / $499. Upgrade prices (revised by the user on 2026-09-14): Standard to Premium $150, Standard to Black $350, Standard to Signature $499, Premium to Black $200, Premium to Signature $499, Black to Signature $400. An upgrade is the same card with the new plan's limits; on an upgrade to Black or Signature the physical card is added and issued alongside the existing virtual card; from Black to Signature the client keeps the plastic card and the metal card is added (Product, Limits and Fees, Physical Cards, FAQ). - Other fees (audit of 2026-09-22): a fixed $0.30 fee on every card transaction; a cross-border fee of about 0.5% when the merchant is outside the card's issuing jurisdiction or the payment is not in USD; a card with no activity for 30 days is frozen for security and unfrozen via support; after 12 months of inactivity a fee of up to $5 per month may apply.
